How every project moves

  1. 1

    Diagnose

    Find out what is wrong.

    We look at the fault or the problem first, and explain it in plain words before we change anything.

  2. 2

    Mend

    Fix it, or build it, carefully.

    Repairs and software are done step by step, using parts and tools we trust.

  3. 3

    Check

    Test it before it goes back to you.

    We test the work end to end, then explain what we did and what to watch for.

Six principles we hold ourselves to

  1. 01

    Quote before work

    You know the price and the plan before we start, and you decide whether to go ahead.

  2. 02

    Plain English

    No jargon. If we use a technical word, we explain it.

  3. 03

    Careful work

    Everything we repair or build is tested before it goes back to you.

  4. 04

    Your data is yours

    We collect only what we need to do the job, and we keep it safe.

  5. 05

    Honest advice

    If something is not worth repairing or building, we say so.

  6. 06

    One team

    The person who fixes your device and the person who builds your software work together.
