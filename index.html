Common repairs

If your fault is not listed, ask. We will tell you honestly whether we can fix it.

  • Phone screens

    Cracked or dead screens on phones and tablets, replaced and tested.

  • Batteries

    Phone dies at 30%? A new battery brings the day back.

  • Charging ports

    Loose cable, slow or no charging. Cleaned or replaced.

  • Water damage

    Dropped in water? Bring it quickly and we will assess what can be saved.

  • Laptop repairs

    Screens, keyboards, fans, and storage or memory upgrades.

  • Updates and fixes

    Stuck updates, crashing apps, slow devices and factory resets.

  • Virus and malware

    Pop-ups, strange behaviour or a hijacked browser. Cleaned and secured.

    New phone or laptop? We move your photos, contacts and files across.

Questions

Good to know

How much will a repair cost?

It depends on the device and the fault. Tell us the make, model and what is wrong, and we will give you a quote before any work begins. You only go ahead if you are happy with it.

Will I lose my data?

Most repairs, like screens and batteries, do not touch your data. Even so, please back up first if you can. If a repair carries a real risk to your data, such as water damage, we will tell you before we start.

Which devices do you repair?

Most common phones, tablets and laptops. Send us the model and we will tell you straight away whether we can fix it.

Do you build software for businesses too?

Yes. Alongside repairs we build websites, business systems, apps and automation. The software side is run by a working software engineer, so you deal with one business for both.

Where are you based?

We are based in the UK. Get in touch and we will explain how drop-off, collection or postal repairs work for your area.

Something broken, or something to build?

Tell us what you need. We will come back with a clear quote and a plain-English plan.
